Interiors for a house surrounded by greenery, where inside and outside almost merge. In the living areas, plants trail down from the ceiling among natural-fiber pendant lights, and wide openings onto the garden let light and greenery in.

The palette is natural and welcoming: wood, stone, straw and sand-toned fabrics, with the blue of the sofas as a counterpoint. In the bedrooms, upholstered headboards and wooden lattice panels bring texture and calm; in the bathrooms, stone and wood-framed mirrors carry the same language, and the bathtub faces the forest.
