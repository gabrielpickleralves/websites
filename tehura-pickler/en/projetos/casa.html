A house designed as a whole, from the architecture to the interiors. Outside, stone, wood and large expanses of glass form clean volumes; at dusk, the house glows from within and is reflected in the pool.

The living area is one large, continuous space under a wooden ceiling, with kitchen, dining and lounge opening onto the pool and the view of the forest. Exposed brick, the black steel staircase and the plants bring texture and a relaxed feel that carries on upstairs, in bedrooms with a character of their own, wooden floors and bathrooms with black-framed glass.
