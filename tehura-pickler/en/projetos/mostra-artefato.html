A space created for the Artefato exhibition, with nature as its starting point. A long living room, wrapped in light curtains, opens onto a gourmet area under a glass roof, surrounded by plants.

In the living room, a low garden runs along the wall, lit niches hold decorative pieces and a large panel in shades of blue covers the wall. In the gourmet area, the stone floor, the pale stone island, the natural-fiber pendant light and the orange hanging chair invite unhurried gatherings in natural light.
