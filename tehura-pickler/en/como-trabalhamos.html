How we work

[PREENCHER] short introduction to the studio's way of working

Stages

  1. 01

    Initial conversation

    We learn what the project needs to solve: how you want to live or work in the space, the property, the schedule and the budget.

  2. 02

    Preliminary study

    The first ideas for the layout and the volumes, presented in drawings and images so we can discuss them together.

  3. 03

    Schematic design

    The approved study gains precision: plans, sections, materials and the first technical decisions.

  4. 04

    Construction documents

    The complete set of drawings and specifications to price and build the work, coordinated with the engineering projects.

  5. 05

    Construction follow-up

    Site visits so that what was designed is built as planned.
